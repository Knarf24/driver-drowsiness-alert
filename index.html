Everything runs on your device. No video leaves your browser.

How it works

1. Find the face

Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker tracks 478 points on your face from the webcam, about 30 times a second.

2. Measure the eyes

The eye aspect ratio (height of the eye divided by its width) drops when the eye closes. Short dips count as blinks; long dips trigger the alarm.

3. Watch the head

The 3D head pose shows a nodding or sideways-slumping head. Mouth opening detects yawns, and a missing face means you looked away.

4. Wake you up

A loud siren plus a spoken warning from your browser's text-to-speech, and the screen flashes red.

Built by combining four GeeksforGeeks Python projects, rebuilt for the browser: Face Detection with Webcam, Eye Blink Detection, Determine Face Tilt and Convert Text to Speech.

This is a demo and a safety aid, not a substitute for rest. If you feel tired, pull over.
